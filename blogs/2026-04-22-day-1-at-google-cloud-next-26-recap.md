<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><h3><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">1. Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform</span></h3>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform is where you go to build, scale, govern, and optimize agents. As the evolution of Vertex AI, it’s built on top of our leading infrastructure, and deeply integrated with our data and security capabilities — the foundation of the Agentic Enterprise. Here’s a sampling of Agent Platform’s new features and capabilities:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Build:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Choose the right environment for the job — from the low-code, visual interface of the new </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Studio</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, to the code-first logic of the upgraded </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Development Kit (ADK)</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">. We’ve simplified the entire lifecycle with AI-native coding capabilities to help you ship production-grade agents faster.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Scale:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Clear the path to production with the re-engineered </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Runtime</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">. This supports long-running agents that maintain state for days at a time and are backed by </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Memory Bank</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> for persistent, long-term context.</span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Govern:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Establish centralized control with </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Identity</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Registry</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, and </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Gateway</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">. These capabilities help ensure every agent — whether built on Agent Platform or sourced from our partner ecosystem — has a trackable identity and operates within enterprise-grade guardrails. </span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Optimize:</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> Guarantee quality with </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Simulation</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Evaluation</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, and </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Agent Observability</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">. These tools provide full execution traces and a real-time lens into agent reasoning to help ensure your agents always hit their goals.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="public-DraftStyleDefault-block public-DraftStyleDefault-ltr"><span>3. AI Hypercomputer</span></h3>

<div class="block-paragraph_advanced"><p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">First, there’s the eighth-generation Tensor Processing Unit, or TPU — “a thing of beauty,” Amin said. And because “the demands of training and serving have completely diverged,” this TPU family actually consists of two chips: </span></p>
<ul>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">TPU 8t, optimized for training</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, uses new Inter-Chip Interconnect (ICI) technology to scale up to 9,600 TPUs and 2 petabytes of shared, high-bandwidth memory in a single superpod. It achieves three times the processing power of Ironwood and delivers up to double the performance per watt. </span></p>
</li>
<li style="vertical-align: baseline;">
<p><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">TPU 8i, optimized for inference</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, uses the new Boardfly topology to directly connect 1,152 TPUs in a single pod. It features three times more on-chip SRAM compared to previous versions and a specialized Collectives Acceleration Engine offloads resource-heavy tasks. Taken together, TPU 8i delivers 80% better performance per dollar for inference than the prior generation, helping </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/compute/tpu-8t-and-tpu-8i-technical-deep-dive"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">millions of concurrent agents to run cost-effectively</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">AI Hypercomputer also supports Arm-based Google Cloud Axion processors, such as the </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">N4A, now generally available</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">, and will be one of the first platforms to offer </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">NVIDIA’s Vera Rubin NVL72</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> platform when it is released.</span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Other parts of the network need to keep up with the demands of the agentic era. For example, the new </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Virgo Network</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> doubles connectivity to scale training beyond AI Hypercomputer superpods, and </span><strong style="vertical-align: baseline;">Google Cloud Managed Lustre</strong><span style="vertical-align: baseline;"> now supports an industry-leading 10 terabytes per second of throughput. </span></p>
<p><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">Learn more about all of our AI infrastructure innovations </span><a href="https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/compute/ai-infrastructure-at-next26?e=48754805"><span style="text-decoration: underline; vertical-align: baseline;">here</span></a><span style="vertical-align: baseline;">. </span></p>
