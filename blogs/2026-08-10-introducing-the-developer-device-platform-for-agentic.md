Most enterprises connect with their customers through a device. Whether it’s using a mobile app to order a product, contact customer service, view content, or manage their account, the customer experience depends on how well an app can run locally on the customer’s device. For this reason, building and testing applications across a wide variety of devices is critical for any enterprise launch that involves locally running components.
